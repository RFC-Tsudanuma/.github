### About Us

**RFC-Tsudanuma** is an independent Japanese team competing in the RoboCup Humanoid League. We are a mixed group of volunteer students and professional engineers, unaffiliated with any specific university or corporation. We have come together with a shared ambition: to build the world's best soccer humanoid robot. Although we are a newly established team, we are aiming for the pinnacle of RoboCup by actively integrating cutting-edge technologies.
  
**RFC-Tsudanuma** は、RoboCup Humanoid League に参戦する日本の独立チームです。特定の大学や企業を代表しない、有志の学生と社会人エンジニアが **「世界一のサッカーヒューマノイドロボットを作る」** という共通の目標で集まりました。設立間もないチームですが、最新の技術を積極的に取り込んでRoboCupの頂点を目指します。
